# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be safe, useful, and respectful. Contributors must communicate with care, critique ideas rather than people, respect stated boundaries, and make room for people with different levels of experience and different backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person’s private information, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review threads, community events, and private communication undertaken in a project role.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s private contact method. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect confidentiality where practical, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

Maintainers must recuse themselves from handling a report when they are directly involved. Good-faith reports and appeals will not be punished.
